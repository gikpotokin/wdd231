NP Membership Benefits

Designed for nonprofit organizations that want to connect with the Lagos business community.

  • No membership fee.
  • Access to selected chamber events.
  • Networking opportunities.
  • Community development opportunities.

Bronze Membership Benefits

A practical membership option for small and growing businesses.

  • Access to networking events.
  • Business training opportunities.
  • Member directory listing.
  • Discounts on selected chamber events.

Silver Membership Benefits

Increased visibility and opportunities for businesses seeking stronger connections.

  • All Bronze Membership benefits.
  • Priority networking opportunities.
  • Business training and workshops.
  • Featured business promotion opportunities.
  • Discounts on selected chamber events.

Gold Membership Benefits

Our premium membership level provides the highest level of visibility, networking, and promotional opportunities.

  • All Silver Membership benefits.
  • Premium business directory placement.
  • Home page spotlight opportunities.
  • Premium advertising opportunities.
  • Priority access to special events.
  • Exclusive leadership and networking opportunities.
